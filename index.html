❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the available training packages?
Standard training is ₹3,500 for 20 days, Premium training is ₹4,500 for 30 days, and Personal 1-on-1 training is ₹8,000 for 30 days. Contact us to confirm current availability and package details.
How can I book a driving class?
Choose a package and tap its booking button. Enter your name and mobile number, then continue to WhatsApp to send your enquiry. Your booking is not confirmed until the class details are agreed with the school.
Can I ask about class timings before admission?
Yes. Use the WhatsApp or call button to ask about available timings, training schedules and the admission process.
Do you provide DL / LL assistance?
DL / LL guidance is listed in the Premium and Personal packages. Ask us which assistance is available. A driving course does not guarantee a driving licence; eligibility, testing and issuance are subject to the relevant RTO requirements.
Where are you located?
Our location search is set to Baripada, Odisha. Tap Open Maps above and verify the exact business pin or call before visiting.
How do I check my student profile?
Tap My Profile in the top navigation or Open My Profile in the Student Dashboard section. This opens profile.html, which must exist in your website.
